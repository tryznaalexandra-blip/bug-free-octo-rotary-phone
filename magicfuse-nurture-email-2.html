Scope & estimates

One thing worth pressure-testing before restarting any Salesforce initiative:

Hi %FIRSTNAME%,

Many estimates look reasonable at first, but change later because the scope wasn’t defined in Salesforce terms.

The risk usually isn’t the hourly rate — it’s assumptions around:

• which processes are truly in scope for phase one

• what data needs to be cleaned or migrated

• which integrations are required now vs. later

• who owns approvals for fields, flows, roles, and reporting

• how much testing and user adoption support is actually needed

When these inputs are unclear, vendors estimate differently — and the cheapest proposal can become the most unpredictable one.

A useful rule of thumb: before comparing estimates, compare the assumptions behind them.
