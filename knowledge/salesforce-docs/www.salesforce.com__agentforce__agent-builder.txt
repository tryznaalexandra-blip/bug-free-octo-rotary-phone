Tell us about your business needs, and we’ll help you find answers. Contact us AI Agent Builder FAQs What is an AI agent builder? An AI agent builder is a tool that simplifies the creation and deployment of AI agents , like Salesforce‘s Agentforce Builder. It‘s a user-friendly, low-code and pro-code tool to manage data sources, create jobs to be done and actions, and monitor agent performance. Learn more about choosing the best AI agent builder . How much does Agentforce Builder cost? Agentforce Builder does not cost anything and is included with the purchase of Agentforce . The AI assistance inside Agentforce Builder is included, and has no cost to use within the builder. Who has access to Agentforce Builder? The Agentforce Builder is located inside Agentforce Studio in the App Launcher. Access is granted by admin permissions. What can I connect to? You can connect Agentforce agents to the Salesforce tools and data you already use — like Flows, Apex, MuleSoft APIs, CRM data, and knowledge articles. Agents can also integrate with external systems, apps, and channels, giving them the context and actions they need to deliver accurate, end-to-end experiences. Are there any pre-built AI agent templates? Yes. Agentforce comes with a library of prebuilt agent templates you can use right away, including the Service Agent, Employee Agent, Campaign Agent, Sales Development Rep, Sales Coach, Guided Shopping (B2B & D2C), Guided Shopping (B2C), and Setup Agent templates. Each can be customized with your own data, guardrails, and business logic to fit your exact needs. Learn more about how to build an AI agent .
