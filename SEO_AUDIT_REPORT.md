## 1. Executive Summary

MagicFuse is a Salesforce-focused agency generating **84 total clicks** over the last 7 days from **42,198 impressions** — a blended CTR of **0.20%**. The site is indexed and ranking for hundreds of high-intent commercial keywords, but the vast majority of traffic is being lost at the SERP level: nearly **99.8% of impressions result in no click**.

**The core problem is not visibility — it is conversion from impression to click.** Average positions are largely sitting between 15–60, meaning the site appears on page 2–6 for most queries. Fixing this requires a combination of on-page optimization, content updates, and — critically — authoritative link building to push pages from position 8–20 into the top 5 where CTR jumps from ~1% to 5–15%.

### Key Metrics at a Glance

| Metric | Last 7 Days | Previous 7 Days | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Clicks | 84 | 76 | +10.5% |
| Total Impressions | 42,198 | 41,086 | +2.7% |
| Average CTR | 0.20% | 0.18% | +0.02pp |
| Average Position | 20.60 | 23.37 | **+2.77 (improving)** |

Positive trend: clicks are up, position is improving. The foundation is there. Acceleration requires targeted link building and CRO on high-impression/low-CTR pages.

## 3. Device Performance Analysis

| Device | Clicks | Impressions | CTR | Avg Position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop | 70 | 38,651 | **0.18%** | 19.19 |
| Mobile | 14 | 3,482 | **0.40%** | 30.21 |
| Tablet | 0 | 65 | 0% | 35.35 |

### Findings

- **Desktop dominates** at 83% of all clicks and 91.6% of impressions — this is expected for a B2B Salesforce agency. Buyers research on desktop.
- **Mobile CTR is higher** (0.40% vs 0.18%) despite a worse average position (30 vs 19). This suggests mobile searchers who find the site are more motivated. However, mobile impressions are only 8.2% of total, meaning the site does not rank well for mobile queries.
- **Mobile position of 30.21** is very poor. This likely indicates mobile Core Web Vitals issues or poor mobile UX that suppresses rankings on mobile. A technical mobile audit is needed.
- **Tablet has zero clicks** from 65 impressions — likely a rendering/UX problem on tablet viewports.

### Recommendations
- Run Google PageSpeed Insights on mobile for key landing pages
- Check mobile usability in GSC Coverage report
- Ensure all service/hire pages are mobile-responsive and fast
- Desktop titles/metas need CTR optimization (they are getting impressions but not clicks)
